# Part 3 · Upgrading the Old Methods with AI

> None of the old roads have disappeared; only the way you walk them has changed.

Advertising, outreach, platforms, partners, content, phone — all of these channels are still in use, and buyers still show up in these places. What changed is the process and the price: targeting has been handed over to the systems, creative capacity has become the competitive edge, and the receiving end and the regulators have tightened the gate at the same time.

This part is ordered by **which step the buyer has reached**, down one road to the end:

- **Step 1 · Find** (Chapters 9–13) — get the buyer to run into you for the first time: buy position, borrow platforms, borrow partners, produce your own content, reach out yourself
- **Step 2 · Shortlist** (Chapters 14–16) — he starts comparing, and you have to be kept: read first, try second, ask third
- **Step 3 · Close** (Chapters 17–18) — he's willing to talk: catch the leads and the conversation, and hold the red lines at the same time
- **Step 4 · Bring the next one** (Chapter 19) — he became a customer, and brings the next customer over
