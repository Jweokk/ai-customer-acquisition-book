# Part 1 · See the Battlefield

> First confirm one thing — where buyers are actually choosing right now, and whether you're present there.

This part has only one chapter, but it decides whether everything you read after it has anything to do with you.

Buyers often have the list already drawn up before they meet you: they ask an AI first, look at other people's reviews first, run a round of comparison themselves first. You can't see these steps, and you can't appeal them. So the first thing to do isn't to learn methods — it's to run a gateway audit and get clear on whether you're present: measure that you are, and you follow the map for the rest of the book; measure that you aren't, and you know which lesson you need to make up.
