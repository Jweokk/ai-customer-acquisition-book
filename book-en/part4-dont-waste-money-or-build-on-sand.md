# Part 4 · Don't Waste Money or Build on Sand

> Whether the two parts above can run at all depends on this one.

This part is not on the buyer's journey. The buyer won't look at you once more because you did the things here — but every method in the previous 19 chapters rests on this part holding true.

It governs five things: **the books** (whether the money you spent actually did anything), **dependency** (none of the gateways the buyer sits in belong to you), **the foundation** (first-party data, compliance, omnichannel orchestration), **people** (who ends up doing all of the above), and **red lines** (the mistakes others have already paid the price for on your behalf).

Reading this part before you act is far cheaper than acting first and coming back to patch it up.
