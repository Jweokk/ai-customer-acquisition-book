# Part 2 · New Methods for the AI Era

> These are the roads buyers have just been given — you have to hold them first.

Buyers now ask AI outright "which one should I choose", let an agent do the filtering for them, look at what platforms push and at what other people say. These roads didn't exist before AI spread, or cost so much nobody walked them — so everything this part covers is a **newly added action**: not doing the old methods better, but building visibility from zero in a new place.

This part covers the first two steps of the main line: where the buyer chooses (Find), and on what grounds he keeps you on the list (the first half of Shortlist).
